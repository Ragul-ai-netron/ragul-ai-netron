# 🚀 FEATURED PROJECTS

## 🛡️ ShieldKnot AI --- Fraud Spike Intercept

A defense-focused fraud-risk operations prototype designed around
detecting emerging fraud spikes and supporting evidence-backed
investigation.

### What it does

-   📥 Ingests and scores transactions
-   📈 Detects abnormal fraud-risk spikes
-   🔎 Investigates channel, device, tenure, geography, velocity and
    amount patterns
-   🤖 Uses specialized investigation agents
-   💡 Generates evidence-backed defensive recommendations
-   🔐 Uses deterministic policy safeguards
-   👤 Keeps final actions behind human authorization
-   📊 Records operational outcomes and verified financial impact

### Evaluation metrics

**Held-out metrics:** - Precision: **0.87** - Recall: **0.92** - F1:
**0.89** - Average false-positive cost: **₹400**

> ShieldKnot AI is designed as a defense-only system. Auto-block is
> disabled by default, actions require human authorization, and a
> deterministic policy layer acts as the final gate.
